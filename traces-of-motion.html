Traces of Motion

Featuring works by Steffen Köhl

Painted Movements – Painted Narratives

Exhibition
28.11.2026 – 10.01.2027
Location
Rotebühlstraße 87, Stuttgart
Vernissage
28.11.2026, 3–8 pm

We are delighted to present the artist and internationally renowned automotive designer Steffen Köhl with his exhibition TRACES OF MOTION at 87artspace in Stuttgart.

From the precision of automotive design to the freedom of artistic expression.

Steffen Köhl explores the emotional interplay of form, body and material in motion. His works translate decades of creative experience into a distinctive visual language in which dynamic compositions and painted narratives come to life.

An afternoon in dialogue with the artist

Steffen Köhl will be present in person at the vernissage and will share insights into his inspirations and the stories behind his works.

We warmly invite you to discover TRACES OF MOTION and to meet the artist in person.

About the artist

Steffen Köhl

Steffen Köhl studied industrial and automotive design at the Fachhochschule für Gestaltung in Pforzheim before beginning his internationally successful career at Mercedes-Benz Design. Over more than three decades he played a decisive role in shaping numerous vehicles.

From 2009 to 2024 he headed Mercedes-Benz Advanced Design, where he was also responsible for creative projects and collaborations at the intersection of design, art and culture – including with the Pinakothek der Moderne in Munich, Berlin Fashion Week and Art Basel Miami Beach.

Painting and illustration remained his own passion all those years, one he continually developed. Today his distinctive works reflect this enthusiasm for aesthetics, movement and visual storytelling.

Köhl’s naturalistic depictions bring hidden layers to the fore and surprise with their own view of people, machines and more.

His artistic roots go back generations: Steffen Köhl is the great-grandson of the German painter Otto Ewel and the great-nephew of the sculptor Maria Ewel.
