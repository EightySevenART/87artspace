FAKE FAKE

Featuring works by Georg Barinov

Exhibition
Until 27.08.2026
Location
Rotebühlstraße 87, Stuttgart
Finissage
27.08.2026, 6–9 pm

In FAKE FAKE, Georg Barinov takes the signs we all know, logos, flags and icons, and rebuilds them in unexpected materials, often from objects others have thrown away.

Crocodiles on worn carpets, a portrait decorated with sportswear logos instead of medals, a flag made of leather and car tyres arranged into rings: familiar symbols, made strange enough to look at twice.

About the artist

Georg Barinov

Georg Barinov was born in 1988 in St Petersburg, then Leningrad. At four he moved with his mother, a stage designer at the Staatstheater, to Stuttgart, where painting was part of everyday life from the start.

His work is a dialogue between two worlds: the scarcity of the Soviet Union, where things were reused again and again, and the abundance and throwaway culture of the West. Moving between pop art and street art, he brings together Western icons such as Donald Duck or the US flag and Russian ones such as matryoshka dolls, often in collages of everyday objects salvaged from scrapyards.

Barinov is a doctor and artist based in Stuttgart and part of the collective Plattform 11, which supports young art in the city. His work has been shown in Stuttgart, Luxembourg, Florence and Munich.

In his own words

What others throw away says a lot about our society. To me, it is never just waste.

In the Soviet Union, consumer goods were rare and used many times over. In Germany, I found an overwhelming abundance and a throwaway society.

Georg Barinov
