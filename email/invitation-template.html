Step into a world where architecture, imagination and memory intertwine.

Sante Muro's paintings open onto dreamlike spaces: arches and stairways, pools and checkerboard floors, populated by wild animals, figures from antiquity and icons of pop culture. Join us for the opening night and meet the artist.

 
About the artist
Sante Muro
Born in 1978 in Polla, Italy. A self-taught painter and muralist, he creates dreamlike visions of interiors between structure and imagination.
