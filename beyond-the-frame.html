About the artist

Sante Muro

Sante Muro was born in 1978 in Polla (Salerno, Italy). He lives in Satriano di Lucania (Potenza) and works in his studio in Potenza.

A self-taught painter, his love of art has kept him painting constantly. He began with the study of classical and contemporary portraiture and moved from textured urban landscapes to his current dreamlike visions of interiors.

He is also a muralist, with large-scale public works in several towns across Basilicata, and has taken part in numerous group and solo exhibitions in Italy and around the world.

In his own words

I am an Italian contemporary artist with an unusual background: I was originally trained as an engineer.

This dual identity shapes my artistic language, combining precision in space, perspective and architecture with the freedom of imagination and emotion. My work explores the balance between structure and dream, often transforming familiar elements such as architecture, water, animals and human figures into symbolic narratives.

What fascinates me most is the moment when the ordinary becomes unexpected, when landscapes become psychological spaces and architecture turns into memory.

My inspiration comes from many different sources, including music, cinema, photography and contemporary visual culture. The Mediterranean landscape is a constant presence in my work, not as a real place, but as a landscape filtered through memory and personal perception.

I am particularly interested in how emotion, imagination and experience can transform reality, creating visual spaces where viewers are invited to find their own meanings and connections.

Sante Muro
