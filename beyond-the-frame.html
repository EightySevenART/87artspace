Exhibition

Step into a world where architecture, imagination and memory intertwine.

Sante Muro's paintings open onto dreamlike spaces: arches and stairways, pools and checkerboard floors, populated by wild animals, figures from antiquity and icons of pop culture.

About the artist

Sante Muro

Sante Muro was born on 23 March 1978 in Polla (Salerno, Italy). He lives in Satriano di Lucania (Potenza) and works in his studio in Potenza.

A self-taught painter, his love of art has kept him painting constantly. He began with the study of classical and contemporary portraiture and moved from textured urban landscapes to his current dreamlike visions of interiors.

He is also a muralist, with large-scale public works in several towns across Basilicata, and has taken part in numerous group and solo exhibitions in Italy and around the world.
