In short

This website does not use cookies, tracking or analytics tools. Our fonts are hosted on our own server, so no data is sent to font providers.

Hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact by email

If you email us, for example to book the space, we process your email address, your name and the content of your message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for booking enquiries, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our mailbox is provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. We delete your data once your request has been dealt with, unless we are legally required to keep it.

Vernissage invitations (newsletter)

If you sign up for our invitations, we use your email address only to send you invitations to our vernissages. Your consent is the legal basis (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). We use the double opt-in procedure: after signing up you receive an email asking you to confirm. We store the time of sign-up and confirmation to be able to prove your consent.

The invitations are sent with Brevo, a service of Sendinblue SAS, 106 boulevard Haussmann, 75008 Paris, France, which stores and processes the email addresses on our behalf. We do not analyse whether invitations are opened or links are clicked.

You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email or by writing to us. We then delete your address from the list.

Storage in your browser

When you open an entry in our archive, your browser temporarily remembers your position in the archive so the "Back to archive" button can return you there. This information stays on your device, contains no personal data and is deleted when you close the tab.

Links to other websites

We link to Instagram and Google Maps. No data is sent to these services until you click such a link; after that, their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your data, to data portability, and to object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). You can withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future. To exercise your rights, simply email us.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg).
